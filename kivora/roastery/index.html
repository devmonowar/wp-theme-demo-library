Brackenmoor Roastery

A small coffee roastery that supplies cafés, delis and offices. The long version of a business site: ten sections on the homepage, a published price list, a services page with a child page under it, and a grid of the shops it supplies.

How to import

  1. Install and activate Kivora. No other plugin is required for the demo itself.
  2. Install One Click Demo Import — the free importer this demo is delivered through.
  3. Go to Appearance → Import Demo Data, find Brackenmoor Roastery, and click Import. Pages, posts, comments, menus, widgets, theme settings, the colour scheme and every image arrive together.

Import onto a fresh site where possible. The importer adds content rather than replacing it, so on a site that already has pages you will end up with both.

What the import creates

  • PAGES

    A homepage with ten sections, and eight pages behind it

    Hero, what we do, why accounts stay, a grid of past work, headline numbers, a three-column price list, quotes, the latest writing, a row of customer logos and a closing call to action — ten block patterns from the theme, each one editable or removable in the ordinary page editor. Behind it: About, Services with a Wholesale coffee page nested under it, Stockists, Pricing, Journal, Contact and a privacy policy.

  • COLOUR

    The demo that changes the theme's colours

    This is the only demo that names a style variation. On import the site switches to Forest — deep green and amber instead of the theme's default navy — which is a one-line setting in the demo rather than anything you have to do afterwards. Global styles you have already edited by hand are left alone.

  • JOURNAL

    Six posts a real trade would write

    Three categories, tags, excerpts, featured images, one sticky post and a thread of comments — enough to see the post grid, the featured post above it, the single-post layout, the author box, related posts and the comment form with real writing in them.

  • SETTINGS

    Menus, widgets and the theme's own options

    A main menu with a submenu and a footer menu, the sidebar and all four footer columns, and the settings this demo uses: sticky header on, sidebar on the right, no sidebar on a single post, live search off. Set deliberately unlike the other two demos, so the three together show what those controls do.

Images

The sixteen photographs are CC0 1.0 and safe to keep on your own site. Photographer credits are listed in CREDITS.md. Nobody is identifiable in any of them, and the roastery, the town, the customers and the prices are fictional. The logo, the site icon and the five customer wordmarks were drawn for this demo and carry the same GPL licence as the theme — borrowing a real company's logo for a demo that lands on thousands of sites is not something we would ask anyone to ship.
