Fieldnotes

A personal journal of walking, writing and photographs. Text-led and deliberately quiet: a typographic homepage, eight posts across three categories, a gallery page, and the sidebar on the left.

How to import

  1. Install and activate Kivora. No other plugin is required for the demo itself.
  2. Install One Click Demo Import — the free importer this demo is delivered through.
  3. Go to Appearance → Import Demo Data, find Fieldnotes, and click Import. Pages, posts, comments, menus, widgets, theme settings and every image arrive together.

Import onto a fresh site where possible. The importer adds content rather than replacing it, so on a site that already has pages you will end up with both.

What the import creates

  • PAGES

    A homepage that is mostly words

    A typographic introduction, the three most recent posts, a short about section and a newsletter invitation — four block patterns from the theme, each one editable or removable in the ordinary page editor. Plus About, a Photographs gallery, Contact and a privacy policy.

  • JOURNAL

    Eight posts, written like a real blog

    Three categories, tags, excerpts, featured images, one sticky post and a thread of comments — enough to see the post grid, the featured post above it, the single-post layout, the author box, related posts and the comment form with real writing in them.

  • SETTINGS

    Menus, widgets and the theme's own options

    A main menu and a footer menu, the sidebar and three of the four footer columns — so you can see the fourth collapse — and the settings this demo uses: sidebar on the left, no sidebar on a single post, live search on, no sticky header. Set deliberately unlike the Creative Studio demo, so the two together show what those controls do.

Images

All ten photographs are CC0 1.0 and safe to keep on your own site. Photographer credits are listed in CREDITS.md. Nobody is identifiable in any of them, and the author, the comments and the places are fictional.
