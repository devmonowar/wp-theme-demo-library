NewCar Repair

Car Repair Services

A full workshop site: services, pricing, the technicians, testimonials, FAQs and a contact page with a booking form.

How to import

  1. Install and activate PortoLite, then install the plugins it asks for — Advanced Custom Fields PRO is required before importing, or the page sections arrive without their content.
  2. Go to Appearance → Import Demo Data in wp-admin.
  3. Find Car Repair Services and click Import — pages, posts, team members, menus, widgets, Customizer settings and every image are added automatically.

What the import creates

  • PAGES

    Three homepages and the inner pages

    Home, Home Classic and Home Centered, plus About, Services, Contact and the blog. Every page is built from the theme's own sections, so each one is editable in the page editor rather than locked into a builder.

  • TEAM

    Six technicians, with their own pages

    Each member carries a role, photo, contact details, biography, skill bars and social links, listed on the team archive and on a page of their own.

  • SETTINGS

    Menus, widgets and the Customizer

    The main menu, the blog sidebar and the four footer columns, plus the brand colour, header and footer styles the demo uses — so the site you land on is the site in the screenshots.
